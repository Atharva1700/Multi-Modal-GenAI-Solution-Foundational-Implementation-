Q3 Regional Sales Report
Prepared by: Dana Whitfield, Operations
Date: 2026-09-30

Summary
Total revenue for Q3 reached $4.2 million, up 12 percent from Q2. The Midwest
region led growth at 18 percent, driven by the Chicago and Milwaukee offices.

Key facts
- The Chicago office closed 46 enterprise contracts.
- Support tickets fell 9 percent after the August release.
- The Denver office remains below target at 84 percent of quota.

Next steps
A regional review is scheduled for 2026-10-20. Dana Whitfield will present
the revised Denver recovery plan.
